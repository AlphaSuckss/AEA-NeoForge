# Third-party code and assets

AEA is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`). It includes material from these MIT-licensed projects; their notices ship in the jar as `LICENSE_*`:

- **Sync 1.11.8** by Overgrown: ported power, action and condition types and textures (`LICENSE_sync`).
- **Origins 1.10.2** by apace100: built-in power data, badges, tags, language files and GUI textures (`LICENSE_origins`).
- **Apoli 2.9.2** by apace100: legacy types and language files (`LICENSE_apoli`).
- **Alternate Origin GUI 1.1.5** by UltrusBot: the origin grid on the choose-origin screen and its texture (`LICENSE_altorigingui`).

Overgrown's Apoli and Origins for NeoForge are dependencies, not included.
